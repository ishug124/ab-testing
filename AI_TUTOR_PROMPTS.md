# 25 prompts for your AI tutor

Use one at a time. The AI is a study partner; check important choices against the course sources.

1. Teach me A/B testing as a beginner. I do not know how to code. Use plain English first, then give the technical word and define it. Ask one question at a time.
2. Explain [concept] with an everyday example, one common mistake, and one question for me.
3. Ask me to explain [concept] in my own words. Wait for me to finish before correcting me.
4. Is this a fair experiment? [Describe it.] Ask about assignment, exposure, and whether people affect each other.
5. Help me choose one primary metric, two driver metrics, and two guardrails for [change].
6. Turn this idea into a testable prediction tied to a decision: [idea].
7. Explain random assignment with a coin flip, including why small groups can still look unbalanced.
8. Explain percentage points versus percent lift using a rate that moves from 10% to 12%.
9. Explain sample size, power, and MDE with simple numbers.
10. Explain this confidence interval in plain language: [paste it]. What does it not tell me?
11. Explain this p-value without saying it is the probability the hypothesis is true: [paste it].
12. My planned split was [split], observed counts were [counts]. What should I check for SRM?
13. Why can stopping a fixed-end test when it first looks positive mislead me?
14. I looked at [number] metrics and [number] groups. How do I report the result honestly?
15. Could this pattern be novelty or seasonality? [Describe the timeline.]
16. Why can clicks per view need a different uncertainty calculation from the share of people who clicked?
17. Pretend I have a surprisingly large lift. Ask me for validity checks one at a time.
18. Explain this code one line at a time: [paste code].
19. I am stuck on [exercise]. Give one small hint, then wait.
20. Explain this error in ordinary words and suggest one small fix: [paste error].
21. Guide me through a tiny two-group simulation and explain each new Python word.
22. Review my output and explanation: [paste both]. What should I check next?
23. Explain CUPED using last month's activity. Why must it be measured before treatment?
24. Compare an A/B test, cluster test, and switchback for this situation: [describe it].
25. Give me a beginner-friendly interview case, one question at a time, then score metrics, design, evidence, and communication.
