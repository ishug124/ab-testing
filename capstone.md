A learning platform is considering a new reminder message. Your task is to plan a fair test, analyse made-up data, and explain a recommendation.

## Analyse

Open the capstone workbook in Colab. Check group counts first. Then estimate the conversion change, review its uncertainty, check the refund guardrail, and compare the result with the practical threshold you set in advance.

## Report

We tested [change] on [population], assigning [unit] to control or treatment from [start] to [end]. The primary metric was [definition]. Treatment changed it by [absolute effect]; the uncertainty range was [range]. Guardrails were [results]. Validity checks found [issues or none]. We recommend [launch / do not launch / follow up] because [plain-language reason].

## Review guide

A strong answer has a fair design, a useful primary measure, protective guardrails, a careful description of uncertainty, and an honest account of limitations.
